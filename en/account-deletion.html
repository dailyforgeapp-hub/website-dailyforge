Account and Data Deletion for DailyForge

Last Updated: February 8, 2026

This page explains how to delete your DailyForge account and associated data.

Delete your account in the app

If you still have access to the app, use the in-app delete flow:

  1. Sign in to your DailyForge account.
  2. Open the Account screen.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion prompt.

What is deleted

After confirmation, your account is removed and your user data is deleted from our backend systems, including:

  • Account profile data (such as user ID and email address).
  • Habits, progress history, daily plans, and related app data.
  • Stored settings and app-generated personal data associated with your account.

Data that may be retained

Some non-personal or legally required records (for example, transaction records handled by Google Play or RevenueCat) may be retained as required for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, or financial reporting obligations.

If you cannot access the app

If you cannot sign in, email us at dailyforgeapp@gmail.com with subject Account Deletion Request - DailyForge and include the email linked to your account.

Important subscription note

Deleting your DailyForge account does not automatically cancel active subscriptions managed by your app store account. Please cancel recurring subscriptions from Google Play or App Store if needed.

Contact

For questions, contact: dailyforgeapp@gmail.com
